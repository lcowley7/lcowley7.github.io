Lachlan Cowley

John Monash Scholar (2027)

I am an Honours student in computer science at Monash University, where I work on mechanistic interpretability with Eduard Hovy and Jey Han Lau. I study how large language models represent what they know: why information is encoded where it is, and how the relationships between concepts shape the layout of a model's representations.

Before this I worked on software verification for Artemis II at NASA, quantum error mitigation at the University of Melbourne, and the SpIRIT nanosatellite.

My papers are on Google Scholar.
